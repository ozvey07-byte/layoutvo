Privacy

LayoutVO does not collect personal data, requires no account and contains no ads or tracking.

Your plans, photos and room scans are stored and processed only on your phone and are never sent to a server. If you share a room by link, the plan travels inside the link itself; the browser that opens it does not send it to a server and we do not store it. The camera is used only to scan a room, when you start it.

Purchases are handled by Apple; we have no access to your payment details. The AI model is downloaded from Apple's servers.

Deleting the app deletes all data. Questions: ozvey07@gmail.com
